## 1. Blocker

### B1 — A peer's fresh acceptance of a tightened revision escapes a standing plan rejection that the row says the tightening keeps (plan row of the one reach rule)

**Text.** §5.10 (b), `PLAN_ACCEPTANCE` row (line 834): "a rejection of item revision `r*`, under any objective revision, denies `r*` and every tighter revision (§13.4) that no judge accepted having seen it: a tightening keeps the controls it tightens, so it keeps what was rejected; an acceptance naming `r` claims `r` and, through coverage, the item's current revision `cur`. So a rejection of `r*` reaches an acceptance naming `r` when `r* = r`, or when ... `r ⊑ cur`, `r* ⊑ cur` and `r* ⋢ r`. ... a fresh independent acceptance of a revision at least as tight as `r*` is a new target it does not reach, and restores issuance". §13.4 (line 1493): "unless its judge accepted a revision at least as tight as the rejected one". §0.3 R14: "an acceptance whose judge saw the rejected controls is a new target". §31.6 (V): "a fresh acceptance of `i2` is vetoed too ... after a further tightening to `i3` instead ... a fresh independent acceptance of `i3` ... restores issuance". §10.3 (line 1292): after an adverse answer "the next step is a new candidate or revision (a new target, hence a new obligation)"; §10.2: `PLAN_ACCEPT(items)` is derived for "an item revision not covered by a current plan acceptance".

**Trajectory (B1(a), tighten and re-dispatch).** One item; only contract fields move; every step is a conforming action.

1. Revision `a1` is the item's first revision. `PLAN_ACCEPT(a1)` is derived. An independent qualified reviewer `H` (a human, R3, or an attested-fresh agent) records `PLAN_ACCEPTANCE REJECTED` naming the objective and `a1`, on a ground no tightening touches: for example, the acceptance criteria do not test the stated objective (`acceptance_criteria`, `objective` and the item prose are invariant under `⊑`, §13.4). The obligation closes undischarged; `COORDINATE(plan)` is derived for the principal.
2. The principal (an agent, R1) lowers `budget` by one: revision `a2`, prose byte-identical, `a1 ⊑ a2`. No acceptance covers `a2`, so `PLAN_ACCEPT(a2)` is derived, a new target and a new obligation (§10.3), and the Dispatcher dispatches a fresh reviewer `P`. Nothing in §16.5 carries `H`'s rejection into `P`'s brief: its carried-findings default covers "the earlier iterations' review findings" of a re-review, and a plan acceptance after an amendment is not one.
3. `P` records `ACCEPTED` naming `a2`. Reach test: `r* = a1 ≠ a2`; `a2 ⊑ cur`, `a1 ⊑ cur`, but `a1 ⊑ a2`, so `r* ⋢ r` fails. Not reached.
