### N2 — preserve the positive-only completeness rule explicitly in the Manual and real-owner tests

The current contract is sufficient, but its polarity matters more than the phrase “whole-tree fallback.” In §6.6, `on` is the S0 same-content predicate on a **recorded** basis. The later clause is headed **“standing (permissive use only)”**, and the explanation explicitly says **“everything that keeps it counting is read whatever its standing.”** Consequently completeness currency can narrow a *permissive* matching inference in (1)/(3), or a reading's permissive use/class justification, but cannot withdraw the recorded adverse matches in the universal exclusions (2)/(4).

A review-only probe showed that applying fallback indiscriminately to both polarities would remove a differing old-content run or a yielding current-content run and raise `ev` from `P` to `T` when a completeness judgment is blocked. That interpretation is excluded by the explicit positive-only rule. A second probe retaining recorded matching for adverse reads left `ev=P` in both variants for `current(C)=T` and `F`. These are predicate probes, not a realized SSDS test or a blocker.

The model uses abstract content tuples and does not realize per-observation narrow-basis/whole-tree matching. At the Manual/implementation boundary, name separate positive-use and adverse-use paths or make their argument polarity explicit; exercise a completeness judgment becoming `P`, blocked, revoked and withheld by loss, with an alternate-tree observation differing only outside the recorded narrow basis. Both the differing-old-run and yielding-current-run must continue qualifying the claim. Do not obtain that result by discarding the observation or by making the claim's unrelated exact passing assessment stale. This is a required discriminator for the claimed polarity at later qualification, not a new Phase B blocker or request to add a persistent mechanism.

The support-graph cycle clause likewise needs a real-owner test: apply its explicit fail-closed/integrity treatment before presenting any cyclic support as current. A permissive provisional value alone is not the clause's promised `F`. I have not proved every corrupted-input fixed-point case; this stays with the Manual and qualification closure rather than being declared covered by the small model.

### N3 — diagnostic independence is improved but bounded

Revision 23's `owner_stale` consults the explicit materiality partition (`3` materially repeats the defect of `1`) rather than the candidate's outcome-identity coverage rule. The candidate rule does not read that partition. P11 again covers identical assessor-read outcomes, and REV22 fails P11/P13. This is a useful independent distinction inside the diagnostic.

However, admission, decision classes, human-floor events, per-observation completeness coverage, structural scopes, witness/publisher behavior, external custody and real scientific assessors remain outside this model. It enumerates **honest** readings. It cannot establish the quality of a reader who supplies formally coherent but materially mistaken judgments. The disclosed E3 residual remains; the unchanged depth-5 report is also uncorroborated (§7). Earliest owner: evidence method and §22 real-owner qualification.

### N4 — cost and scientific applicability remain empirical reopen conditions

The new reading is needed per genuinely new outcome per counted assessor-read failure. Whole-tree fallback, non-reproducing scientific outcomes, stochastic/free execution attributes, unavailable external pins and accumulating owner dispositions can therefore be costly. §26 names these conditions and routes reopening. One reading's reuse on an identical outcome is not evidence that the outcome's meaning remains unchanged after the **reading** changes. The coverage rule excludes that reuse.

These are material product uncertainties; no runtime feasibility or burden reduction was measured here. They are not grounds to invent a different architecture or withhold this review's PASS while §21/§25 explicitly place that evidence at later gates.
