4. **The owner exception and the no-eligible-judge route (SD-48).** Whether §5.10 (f) now grants the exception exactly where workplan §6.6 and §6.8 step 3 do (an authenticated owner's disposition of a failing assessment, including where authorship is unknown) and nowhere else; whether the f044820 B1 trajectory and its six siblings (transformation review, subject-triple review, item-level and joint-invariant reviews, conformance, plan acceptance) are now closed; whether the positive disposition case and the non-author and unknown-lineage controls still hold; and whether "the obligation waits and human attention is derived" keeps the no-stranded-obligation invariant (§10.3) without creating a route that lets work proceed on non-independent closure.
5. **The judged-content rule and review targets (SD-47 A).** Whether §5.10 (b), with §3.4's three target types, is one coherent rule read identically by reach, relief and independence; whether it satisfies A5 and A9 on the 9431629 B1 trajectories (empty, partial and all-empty re-dispatch; re-partition) and on the SD-46 diagnostic cases (a part a re-partition replaced; a copy under the same prose and fixed fields); whether it over-excludes judges (a replaced producer, a same-key item in another scope, another item's content in the scope); whether its consequences for drift, cross-plan copies and changed prose are stated (§32.2); and whether "a judgment supports an integration only for the content it judged" is a sound architecture-level form of the catalogue's staleness rules.

7. **Realizability.** Whether each decision here is realizable by some mechanism. The catalogue body and the diagnostics are evidence for this only: a catalogue clause that disagrees with the Manual, or a catalogue mechanism with a counterexample, is a register entry, not a G1 blocker, unless it shows that no mechanism can realize the Manual's decision.
8. **Register completeness.** Whether catalogue C.2 lists every known open mechanism question the earlier Reviews raised and the candidate has not closed at the architecture level. A missing entry is a non-blocking finding; a register entry that is really an architecture defect is a G1 blocker.
